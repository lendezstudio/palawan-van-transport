Travel-day checklist

Know the Details Before You Go

Travel days are easier when you know where to meet, what time to be ready, and which transfer you are taking. Before confirming a trip, make sure you have the latest information for your route.

Final schedules, prices, and policies are subject to confirmation by Palawan Van Transport & Travel Services.

  • Current departure schedule
  • Pickup and drop-off location
  • Shared or private transfer
  • Number of passengers
  • Luggage requirements
  • Current fare and payment instructions
